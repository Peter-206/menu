# The Bar — Cocktail List

Open bar · No prices

Recipes are in serving amounts. Items marked *optional garnish* do not affect menu availability.

## Real Cocktails

### Mimosa

Sparkling wine · orange juice

- 3 oz Sparkling wine
- 3 oz Orange juice

### Lemon Drop

Vodka · lemon · triple sec

- 2 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Lemon juice
- ½ oz Triple sec
- ½ oz Simple syrup

### Whiskey Sour

Whiskey · lemon · simple syrup

- 2 oz Whiskey
- ¾ oz Lemon juice
- ¾ oz Simple syrup

### Mudslide

Vodka · coffee liqueur · Irish cream

- 1 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Coffee liqueur
- 1 oz Irish cream
- 1½ oz Heavy cream
- drizzle Chocolate syrup — optional garnish

### Frozen Mudslide

Coffee liqueur · Irish cream · vanilla ice cream

- 1½ oz Vodka
- 1½ oz Coffee liqueur
- 1½ oz Irish cream
- 3 cups Vanilla ice cream
- 1 cup Ice
- drizzle Chocolate syrup — optional garnish

Method: Blend 20–30 seconds.

### Piña Colada

White rum · pineapple · coconut

- 2 oz White / light rum
- 3 oz Pineapple juice
- 2 oz Coconut cream
- 1 cup Ice

### Strawberry Daiquiri

White rum · strawberry · lime

- 2 oz White / light rum
- 1 oz Lime juice
- 1 oz Simple syrup
- 1½ cups Frozen strawberries

### Margarita

Tequila · margarita mix

- 2 oz Tequila
- 4 oz Margarita mix

### Mojito

White rum · lime · mint

- 2 oz White / light rum
- 1 oz Lime juice
- 1 oz Simple syrup
- ¼ tsp Mint extract

### Sex on the Beach

Vodka · peach · orange · cranberry

- 2 oz Vodka
- 2 oz Peach schnapps
- 2 oz Orange juice
- 1 oz Cranberry juice
- garnish Maraschino cherry — optional garnish

### Tequila Sunrise

Tequila · orange · grenadine

- 2 oz Tequila
- 4 oz Orange juice
- ½ oz Grenadine

### Bahama Mama

Two rums · orange · pineapple

- 1 oz Dark rum
- 1 oz White / light rum
- 1½ oz Orange juice
- 1½ oz Pineapple juice
- ½ oz Grenadine

### Blue Hawaiian

Light rum · blue curaçao · coconut

- 1 oz White / light rum
- 1 oz Blue curaçao
- 2 oz Pineapple juice
- 1 oz Coconut cream
- topping Whipped cream — optional garnish
- garnish Maraschino cherry — optional garnish

### Blue Lagoon

Vodka · blue curaçao · lemonade

- 1 oz Vodka
- 1 oz Blue curaçao
- 4 oz Lemonade

### Long Island Iced Tea

Five spirits · lemon · cola

- ½ oz Vodka
- ½ oz Gin
- ½ oz White / light rum
- ½ oz Tequila
- ½ oz Triple sec
- ¾ oz Lemon juice
- ¾ oz Simple syrup
- to top Cola

### Moscow Mule

Vodka · ginger beer · lime

- 2 oz Vodka
- 4 oz Ginger beer
- ½ oz Lime juice

### Mai Tai

Two rums · curaçao · orgeat

- 1 oz White / light rum
- 1 oz Dark rum
- ¾ oz Orange curaçao
- ¾ oz Orgeat
- 1 oz Lime juice
- 1 oz Pineapple juice
- ½ oz Orange juice

### Jack Daniel’s Summer Peach

Jack Daniel’s · peach · seltzer

- 5 oz Seltzer

### Jack Daniel’s Watermelon

Jack Daniel’s · watermelon · seltzer
